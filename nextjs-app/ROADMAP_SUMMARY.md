# DateNow: Roadmap and Summary

## N. MVP Roadmap (Detailed)

### Sprint 1: Foundation & Psychological Profiling
- **User Authentication**: Secure sign-up/login with NextAuth.js or custom JWT.
- **Onboarding Experience**: 73-question psychological inventory (Big Five, Values, Love Languages).
- **Profile Management**: Photo uploads, bio, and basic preferences.
- **Match Engine**: Initial compatibility algorithm based on psychological data.

### Sprint 2: The AI Moderator & Guided Sessions
- **Gemini 1.5 Integration**: Implementation of the production-grade moderator prompt.
- **Conversation State Machine**: 7-stage session management (Consent → Outcome).
- **Real-time Chat UI**: WebSocket-based or Server Action-polled messaging interface.
- **Safety Pipeline**: Pre-screening and post-processing of AI-facilitated messages.

### Sprint 3: Readiness, Virality & Reveal
- **Readiness Scoring Engine**: Implementation of the weighted readiness factors.
- **Compatibility Reveal Cards**: Dynamic generation of shareable "Connection Journey" assets.
- **Viral Invite System**: Personalized "Guided Intro" links for social sharing.
- **Analytics & Events**: Instrumentation for funnel, activation, and safety metrics.

## R. Strategic Summary
DateNow is positioned to disrupt the dating industry by introducing a new category: **AI-Guided Relationship Readiness**. By moving beyond the "swipe and hope" model, we provide a premium, emotionally intelligent experience that prioritizes safety, depth, and authentic connection.

The combination of a professional AI moderator, deep psychological insights, and sophisticated viral loops creates a product that is not only useful but naturally shareable and culturally resonant.
